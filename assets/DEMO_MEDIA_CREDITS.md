# FocusGUI film — sources and production notes

This is a scripted illustrative simulation, not a recording of a deployed agent. Application interfaces, instructions, action overlays, and outcomes were created for the film. They do not represent experimental results. Stock video is used as content inside the simulated phones; voice instructions and UI sounds are designed audio.

## Stock video

- `ocean.mp4`: [Waves at an empty beach, Mixkit 1930](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/waves-at-an-empty-beach-1930/).
- `coffee.mp4`: [Milk being poured into a coffee cup, Mixkit 4993](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/milk-being-poured-into-a-coffee-cup-4993/).
- `drive.mp4`: [Adventurers enjoying the road, Mixkit 2729](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/adventurers-enjoying-the-road-2729/).
- `city.mp4`: [Coast of a big city, Mixkit 3302](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/coast-of-a-big-city-3302/).
- `stars.mp4`: [Time lapse video of night sky, Pexels 857195](https://www.pexels.com/video/857195/).

The four Mixkit item pages identify the footage as covered by the [Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree), permitting commercial and personal projects. The Pexels footage is covered by the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

## Sound

- Sea ambience: Mixkit wave sound effect 1196, downloaded from the [Mixkit waves sound collection](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/waves/), under the [Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree).
- Voice prompts: local macOS speech synthesis, created specifically for the simulated tasks.
- Tonal score and interface sounds: original procedural synthesis, created specifically for the demo.

The exported MP4 combines five separately panned task audio cues into one designed stereo mix. These are not audio originally recorded with the stock videos.

## Map

The local map image uses four OpenStreetMap tiles around HarbourFront, Singapore: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The route overlay and arrival estimate are illustrative and are not an actual routing calculation.
